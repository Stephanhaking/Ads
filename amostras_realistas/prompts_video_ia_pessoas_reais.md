# Prompts para gerar as cenas com pessoas realistas (ferramenta de vídeo com IA)

Use a foto do inflador (`anuncio/produto_original.jpg`) como imagem de referência do produto em cada cena.
Formato: vertical 9:16, 5–8 s por plano, estilo publicitário, luz natural, câmara à mão leve.
Depois de gerar os planos, eu monto o anúncio final (narração, textos, preço, entrega grátis e botão do WhatsApp).

Personagens (manter iguais em todos os planos):
- HOMEM: negro, cerca de 35 anos, barba curta, polo azul, calças escuras, ténis brancos.
- MULHER: negra, cerca de 30 anos, lenço amarelo na cabeça, vestido de capulana laranja.
- CARRO: SUV vermelho. Cenário: estrada perto de Maputo, Moçambique, palmeiras, fim de tarde (hora dourada).

1. GANCHO (0–4 s)
   "Vertical 9:16 cinematic shot, golden hour on a road near Maputo, Mozambique. A Black man in a blue polo puts both hands on his head looking at the flat rear tire of a red SUV, a Black woman with a yellow headwrap and orange capulana dress stands beside him worried. Close-up of the deflated tire, then his worried face. Photorealistic, natural skin, shallow depth of field, handheld camera."
2. SOLUÇÃO (4–8 s)
   "The same man opens the car trunk and takes out a small black portable tire inflator (match the reference photo exactly, with the digital screen showing 2.50), smiling and holding it to the camera. Photorealistic, golden hour, soft camera movement."
3. DEMONSTRAÇÃO (8–16 s)
   "The same man crouches by the rear wheel and connects the inflator hose to the tire valve; close-up of his hands attaching the nozzle; close-up of the inflator screen lit; the tire slowly gets fuller (not instant). The woman watches and smiles. Photorealistic, natural hands, golden hour."
4. OFERTA (16–24 s)
   "Product hero shot of the same black portable inflator (reference photo) next to a modern red SUV, clean cinematic lighting, slow orbit camera, space on the frame for text."
5. FECHO (24–30 s)
   "The couple smiles beside the inflated tire, the man gives a thumbs up, then the camera ends on the inflator in the foreground with the car behind. Photorealistic, warm sunset light."
Dica: peça ao gerador "no text, no logos" e coloque os textos depois.
