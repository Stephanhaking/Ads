# What If You Woke Up With a Domain Expansion in Real Life? (Jujutsu Kaisen): v01
# Para aprovação antes de voz e visuais. Protocolo: título (escolhido pelo utilizador) + thumbnail ("NO ESCAPE": cúpula vermelha gigante sobre a cidade, boneco de costas de casaco ciano de braços abertos; alternativa "MY WORLD NOW") + só depois o script.
# O que a thumbnail promete e o vídeo cumpre: a barreira vermelha a fechar sobre a cidade (imagem aos ~8 s); "NO ESCAPE" = acerto garantido (sure-hit) e o preço de prender alguém; o casaco ciano contra o vermelho.
# NOVIDADE: etiquetas no ecrã [CANON] (da obra, só com fontes), [OUR RULE] (regra nossa, ficção) e [OUR GUESS] (palpite). A narração diz "In the story" (CANON), "In your version" (OUR RULE) e "Our guess" (OUR GUESS).
# Duração: alvo ~10 min (~1 500 palavras). Abertura: nome do poder aos 0 s, primeira imagem do poder aos ~8 s.
# Factos só de docs/VERIFICACAO_DOMAINEXPANSION_22NOV.md ("Verificado"): barreira + domínio inato + acerto garantido; custo enorme de energia; domínio incompleto perde a garantia; Simple Domain anula o acerto garantido; Malevolent Shrine é um espaço aberto sem barreira fechada; Unlimited Void inunda o alvo de informação até o imobilizar; Miller 1956 (~4 a 9 itens).
# Fora: bloqueio da técnica depois do domínio, "0,2 s" do Gojo, Idle Death Gamble, primeiro domínio, criador da Simple Domain.
# Tudo o que é "técnica inata do utilizador", tamanho, regras de proteção, cenas do mundo = OUR RULE / OUR GUESS (ficção nossa). Sem violência gráfica.

## Cold open (0:00)
[11:20 da noite; um telhado; uma cidade; o boneco de casaco ciano de braços abertos; uma cúpula vermelha a fechar-se sobre os prédios, com um redemoinho no topo]
You've woken up with a Domain Expansion, from Jujutsu Kaisen. In real life.
[CANON] In the story, it's a barrier that closes around a space and makes your technique impossible to miss.
Right now, it's closing over the whole block, and nobody inside can leave.
[OUR RULE] In your version, that's the only rule. No escape. For anyone. Including you.
On day forty, a storm will make that rule the only thing between two hundred children and the sky.
But first, it's 11:20 at night, and the red dome is already closing.

## 11:20 p.m.: the kitchen (0:32)
[a cozinha; um gato do vizinho; fios vermelhos finos a ligar tudo]
[CANON] A domain isn't a spell. It's the user's innate domain, built inside a barrier, and filled with their innate technique.
[OUR RULE] You don't have a technique. So the dome decides for you: yours ties everything inside to you with thin red threads. Whatever's in the dome can't walk out.
Your neighbour's cat is in your kitchen. It has never liked you. Now it's tied to your ankle, and it's furious about it.
[CANON] Inside a domain, the attack can't miss. [OUR RULE] In your version, neither can the thread.
Lesson one: the barrier closes around everyone, not just the people you want.

## 7:10 a.m.: the size (1:09)
[o corredor do prédio; o elevador; uma bolha vermelha que cresce]
In the morning you try it again, on purpose.
Your dome is the size of your flat. Then the stairwell. Then, for ten seconds, the whole building.
[OUR GUESS] Our guess: your range grows with how much you want it to.
The lift stops between floors. A man in a suit stares at the red threads, and then at his shoes.
He says nothing. He just leans on the wall, very carefully, as if the wall might be a part of it.
Lesson two: a bigger domain isn't a better domain. It's just a bigger apology.

## 12:30 p.m.: the meeting (1:44)
[uma sala de reuniões; o teu chefe; um copo de café suspenso]
At lunch, your boss starts a meeting about the budget.
You're nervous. A thin dome closes around the table. Eight people. Eight red threads.
[CANON] In the story, a Simple Domain can neutralise a domain's guaranteed hit. [OUR RULE] In real life, nobody has a Simple Domain.
That means every thread lands. Your boss's. The intern's. Everybody's.
You feel all of them at once. The budget numbers. The intern's panic. Your boss, who is secretly thinking about a sandwich.
Lesson three: when nobody can resist you, being careful is the only defence anyone has.

## Day 3: the incomplete one (2:17)
[a casa de banho; uma cúpula do tamanho de uma banheira; vermelho a falhar]
[CANON] In the story, an incomplete domain loses the guaranteed hit.
So on day three you stop trying to do big ones. You practise in the bathroom.
A dome the size of a bathtub works perfectly. A dome the size of a shed flickers and fails, and the threads go slack.
You eat a whole loaf of bread. Not hungry. Starving.
[CANON] A domain takes an immense amount of cursed energy.
Lesson four: small and complete beats big and broken.

## Day 6: the supermarket (2:46)
[um supermercado; a cúpula a abrir; tu a sentar-te no chão]
On day six, you open a full one in the supermarket, just to see how long you can hold it.
You hold it for forty seconds. Then your legs go. You sit down in front of the frozen peas, and an employee asks if you need a doctor.
You don't tell anyone that the red threads were still tied to a trolley when you passed out.
Lesson five: the bill comes after the show, and it always asks for you.

## Day 10: the world notices (3:16)
[uma esquina; um telemóvel a filmar; uma cúpula vermelha sobre uma loja]
A boy films a red dome over a corner shop. It lasts nine seconds.
The video spreads. Half of the comments say it's an effect. The other half say it's a glitch in the sky.
Nobody says "domain". The only people who know what that means think it's a joke.
Lesson six: the ones who'd understand are the ones who'd never believe it.

## Day 12: the calm one (3:39)
[um estúdio de ioga; uma professora de olhos fechados; um fio vermelho que escorrega]
On day twelve you go to a yoga class, because someone told you it would help with the stress.
The teacher sits cross-legged at the front. You close a small dome around the room, just to test it.
Eleven threads tie on. The twelfth, hers, slides off, like water off a window.
[CANON] In the story, a Simple Domain can cancel a domain's guaranteed hit.
[OUR GUESS] Our guess: someone who is completely calm is the closest thing real life has to one.
She opens one eye. She doesn't say a word. She just smiles, as if she has seen a thousand of these.
Lesson seven: the only defence against "can't miss" is not caring who's watching.

## Day 15: too many threads (4:21)
[uma estação de comboio; muitas linhas vermelhas; tu, de mãos na cabeça]
On day fifteen, you open one in a train station, and for the first time, the threads aren't eight. They're two hundred.
[CANON] In the story, Gojo's Unlimited Void floods the target with information until they can't move.
Yours does it to you. In 1956, the psychologist George Miller showed that people hold only around four to nine things in mind at once.
You're tracking two hundred.
You close the dome, sit on the platform, and don't speak for twenty minutes.
Lesson eight: sure-hit is easy to want. It's hard to carry.

## Day 20: the open one (4:55)
[o teu bairro de cima; uma cúpula que se abre sem fecho; a cidade lá em baixo]
[CANON] In the story, Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine has no closed barrier at all. It's an open space with a huge reach.
[OUR GUESS] Our guess: that's what a domain without walls would feel like.
On day twenty, you try it from the roof. The red doesn't close. It spreads. Over the street, the park, the river, and the city beyond.
Every window is tied to you.
You can feel the whole city's weight on the other end of the threads: the late shift, the baby awake, the dog that won't stop barking.
You close your hand. The dome snaps shut, and the sky is just a sky again.
Lesson nine: if there's no wall, there's no one else to blame.

## Day 25: the apology (5:38)
[um café; o teu chefe; a estagiária; os dois a olhar-te]
On day twenty-five, you go back to the people you tied.
Your boss. The intern. The man in the lift.
You don't explain. You can't. So you buy coffee, and you say that you're sorry, and that you'll make sure it never happens again.
The intern says, "I felt something. I thought it was just a very long Monday."
Your boss says nothing for a moment, and then he tells you what he was really thinking about in that meeting: not a sandwich, but his daughter's birthday, which he'd forgotten.
You'd felt that thread before you ever knew what it was.
Lesson ten: you can't un-feel what you've tied to. You can only decide what to do with it.

## Day 30: the man who wouldn't leave (6:22)
[a tua rua; um homem que tenta sair da cúpula; os fios a esticar]
On day thirty, a stranger breaks into your car. You're still holding a small dome from the evening.
He runs. He hits the edge. He can't leave.
[OUR RULE] In your version, the only door is you.
He's terrified. He doesn't get hurt. You just stand there, a metre from him, holding the walls.
And you realise that "no escape" is a sentence, not a power, and that you're the one who has to decide how long it lasts.
You open it. He runs without looking back.
Your hands are shaking so hard that you have to sit down on the kerb, and you stay there until the streetlights flicker back to their normal colour.
Lesson eleven: the strongest rule you can set is the one you can end.

## Day 35: the rehearsal (7:09)
[um recreio de noite; uma cúpula pequena; gravilha suspensa no ar]
On day thirty-five, you take it to an empty playground at night.
You've never tried to keep something out. Only in.
You close a dome the size of the slide, and throw a handful of gravel at it.
The gravel stops at the wall, and hangs there, as if the air had gone thick.
You stand in the middle, with the pebbles floating around you like a tiny planet, and you laugh. For the first time in thirty-five days, you laugh.
Lesson twelve: the same wall that traps can also protect.

## Day 40: the storm (7:43)
[uma escola; uma tempestade enorme; duzentas crianças; uma cúpula vermelha a cobrir o recreio]
On day forty, a storm tears across the city, and the siren at the primary school goes off.
Two hundred children are in the yard. The nearest shelter is two minutes away.
[OUR RULE] In your version, the dome can keep things in. And it can keep things out.
You run. You spread your arms at the gate. The red closes over the yard, the roof, and the car park.
Wind, rain, branches. A trampoline crosses the road like a leaf. Nothing reaches the children.
Your legs shake. You hold it for four minutes. The longest four minutes of your life.
When the sky clears, you open your hands. Two hundred faces look at a red sky that's turning back into a grey one.
Nobody asks what you are.

## Day 41: the cost (8:30)
[o teu quarto; tu, sentado no chão; um fio vermelho preso ao teu dedo mínimo]
The next morning you can't lift your arms.
There's one red thread still tied to your finger. It's the cat's. It hasn't forgiven you, and it hasn't left either.
You think about the storm, the school, the children, and how none of them will ever know your name.
Lesson thirteen: the bravest use of "no escape" is the one where the only person trapped is you, for as long as it takes.

## Ending (8:57)
[11:20 da noite; o telhado; a cidade; a cúpula a fechar-se de novo]
The next night, at 11:20, you're back on the roof.
So if you woke up tomorrow with a Domain Expansion, what would your technique be?
And would you ever open it all the way, so that the whole city was inside? Tell me in the comments.
