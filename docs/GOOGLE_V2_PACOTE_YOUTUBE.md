## Descrição
A study trained an AI on more than 200,000 hospital stays to answer one question: who in this building is going to die? Google was part of the team. This video follows the question back to the data, and then forward to what it means for the rest of us.

In this video:
• The 2018 study: about 114,000 patients, about 47 billion data points, and what the result actually measured
• Why "anonymous" does not mean what most people think, and two real cases in the US and the UK (a lawsuit dismissed for lack of harm, and a regulator's ruling)
• How an advertising company makes money from predictions, and why certainty is the product
• How YouTube changed what it optimized for, and the internal film "The Selfish Ledger"
• What the 2024 antitrust ruling decided, and what it left untouched

One thing to do this week: open your Google account settings and look at one thing it keeps about you.
