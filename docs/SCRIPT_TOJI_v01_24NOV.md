# What If You Became Toji?: v01
# Para aprovação antes de voz e visuais. Título curto, no formato YuVsReality (decisão do utilizador). Thumbnails recebidas (escolher 1): "BORN TO HUNT" (painéis NO DETECTION, lua vermelha, torii) e "NO CURSED ENERGY" (painel SCAN, CE LEVEL 0.00%, STATUS NONE). O script cumpre as duas: painel de varrimento com 0,00 %, quadros NO DETECTION, caça ao fim da noite, lua vermelha e torii.
# Sem contagem de dias (diferente do Sukuna): uma noite, um dia, uma caçada.
# Etiquetas no ecrã [CANON] / [OUR RULE] / [OUR GUESS]. Narração: "In the story" / "In your version" / "Our guess".

## Cold open (0:00)
[3:07 da manhã; um quarto às escuras; os olhos de um boneco a abrir-se; um painel a vermelho: SCAN, CE LEVEL 0.00%, STATUS NONE]
You've woken up as Toji Fushiguro. In real life.
You have zero cursed energy. Nothing in this world can sense you.
[CANON] In the story, that isn't a weakness. It's the trade he was born with.
[OUR RULE] In your version, there's a curse hunting your best friend tonight. You're the only thing it can't feel coming.
But first, it's 3:07 in the morning, and your hands are shaking, because they are much stronger than they should be.

## 3:07 a.m.: the body (0:40)
[o quarto; o chão a ranger; uma maçaneta dobrada na mão; os sentidos a crescerem]
[CANON] Toji was born with a Heavenly Restriction. It took away all of his cursed energy and gave him a body and senses at the limit.
You sit up and the bed frame cracks. You reach for the light switch and the plate comes off in your hand. Just a plastic plate. You hold it very, very carefully.
Then the sounds arrive. The fridge, the pipes behind the wall, a neighbour's heartbeat three floors down.
[CANON] In the story, his senses are sharp enough to catch curses by tiny changes in the air and the temperature.
You step into the corridor and the air is colder by the stairs. A small, quiet, wrong kind of cold.
[OUR RULE] In your version, that's the curse's trail. Faint. Old. Moving toward Dani's building.
Dani is your best friend. Dani is also the only person you know who can see curses.
You take the stairs. All eleven floors, in under a minute, without being out of breath. You didn't mean to run. Your legs just decided.
On the way down you pass a mirror. You stop.
It's your face. Same tired eyes, same crooked nose. But the way you stand has changed. Quieter. Lighter. Like someone who has already decided where the exits are.
[OUR RULE] In your version, you keep your own face. The body is his, and you're just the one living in it.
You grab your jacket. It's a size too small. You keep it anyway.

## Dani and the scanner (2:15)
[a cozinha da Dani; um scanner de mão com um ecrã a vermelho; o boneco ao lado dela; NO DETECTION em quadros]
Dani opens the door at 3:40 with a mug, half asleep.
"I can't feel you," she says, frowning. "Say something."
"I'm right here."
"I know you're there. I can hear you. But I can't feel you. It's like you're a... hole."
She has a scanner her mother built. A little box that reads cursed energy and shows a number.
It reads the neighbour's cat. It reads Dani. It reads the fridge, a little, because the fridge is old.
You stand next to it and the screen says: CE LEVEL, 0.00 percent. STATUS, none.
[CANON] In the story, Toji was called the invisible man. Nothing could sense him, unless it came from the energy in his tools or a curse he carried.
[OUR RULE] In your version, that's the whole trick. And the whole cost.
Dani looks at the number for a long time.
"Nobody's ever going to be able to find you," she says. "Even if they want to."
You say you don't need to be found. She doesn't answer.
[CANON] In the story, his clan, the Zenin, pushed him out for relying on tools instead of energy.
You think: yes, that sounds right.

## The street (3:30)
[uma rua vazia às 4 da manhã; pequenas maldições a flutuar; o boneco a passar por entre elas]
You walk Dani home through the empty street. She walks half a step behind, scanning every doorway.
The street is full of tiny curses. Little grey knots that drift between the lamp posts, feeding on the loud, tired people who walk by.
They follow Dani like moths. Not one of them turns toward you.
One brushes your shoulder. It doesn't stop. It doesn't hesitate. It simply drifts on, as if you were a piece of the wall.
[OUR RULE] In your version, that's how the world sees you now. Not as an enemy. As nothing at all.
You'd expected to feel powerful. You feel, mostly, a little lonely.
Dani sees your face and says, gently: "It's not a bad thing. It's just a very quiet thing."
At the end of the street, she stops and looks at the shrine on the hill, black against a red sky.
"It's been there for a month," she says. "It eats the loudest person in range. That's me. Tonight, that's me."

## The case (4:20)
[uma caixa preta comprida no armário de uma loja; quatro objetos a brilhar ao de leve]
Dani's mother left something in the storage unit under the shrine. A long black case.
Four tools. You know what they are before you open it.
[CANON] The Inverted Spear of Heaven nullifies a technique the moment it touches it. In the story, it ended Gojo's Infinity.
[OUR RULE] In your version, it only cancels what is active when it touches. It does nothing to a fist. It does nothing to a wall.
[CANON] The Chain of a Thousand Miles gives it reach, like a kusarigama.
You swing it once, in an empty room. It takes the ceiling light with it.
[CANON] The Split Soul Katana cuts the soul and ignores the body's armor.
[OUR RULE] In your version, it can hurt a curse without cutting a single brick. You test it on a mattress. The mattress is fine. You are not.
[CANON] And the Playful Cloud is a special grade tool. A heavy weapon.
You pick it up one-handed. You're careful with this one too.
Dani watches you. "Please tell me you're not going to do the thing."
"What thing?"
"The thing where you go and hunt it."
You tell her the truth. It can't feel you. It can feel her. If she stays home, it comes to her. If she goes with you, she's bait.
"So I'm bait either way," she says.
"Not if I get there first."
It's the least reassuring thing you've ever said. She picks up the scanner anyway.

## The shrine (5:25)
[um torii vermelho; uma escadaria longa; uma lua vermelha; quadros NO DETECTION nos cantos]
The curse is in the old shrine at the end of the line, where the metro turns around.
You can feel it, because your body feels everything. A pulse in the cold, like a slow knock on glass.
Dani stays at the gate with the scanner. Her screen shows a big red number for the curse. And for you, just a quiet grey frame that says NO DETECTION.
[OUR RULE] In your version, that's the rule that makes you dangerous. Not strength. The fact that it never knows you're coming.
You walk up the stairs in silence. Not a single sound.
The curse is waiting at the top. It's turned toward the gate, toward Dani, toward all that warm, loud energy.
It isn't looking at you. You're not there.
[OUR GUESS] Our guess is that a creature that hunts by sensing energy would never be built to expect something without any.
You step into its blind spot, and for one second you are the quietest thing in the world.

## The hunt (6:55)
[o templo; uma cabeça de maldição enorme; a corrente a girar; a lança a tocar um símbolo a brilhar]
It has a technique. It seals the space around the gate, and Dani can't leave.
You didn't plan for that.
You swing the chain. It wraps the ward's anchor. You pull the spear down onto the symbol, and the ward goes out like a lightbulb.
The curse turns. It finally feels something. A shape. Not an energy. Just a man who weighs too much.
Its first strike misses by an inch, because it's aiming at where a person's energy would be, and there isn't any.
Its second misses by less. It's learning.
Its third doesn't miss.
It hits you.
[OUR RULE] In your version, your body is a wall, not a god. It hurts. Something in your ribs says so out loud.
You hit back with the katana, the one that ignores armor. Once. Twice.
The curse falls apart without a sound. No dust. Nothing broken. Just gone.
The red moon is very quiet.
You stand there, breathing, waiting for the feeling you expected. Victory. Pride. Something.
There's only the cold, and the tiredness, and the sound of Dani's footsteps stopping at the gate.
Then your legs stop listening.

## The dark (8:15)
[o boneco no chão, ao pé de um torii; o scanner de Dani na escadaria a mostrar um quadro cinzento: NO DETECTION; vozes a gritar um nome]
You fall at the foot of the torii, behind a pillar, and nobody knows.
Dani is calling your name. Her scanner shows the curse is gone, and it shows nothing else.
It shows no one.
[CANON] In the story, that's what made Toji so frightening. Nothing could find him.
[OUR RULE] In your version, that's exactly what's about to kill you.
Dani throws the scanner down. She stops trying to feel you.
She starts listening instead.
She hears the chain, dragging softly against the stone, as you breathe. She hears your breath, which is thin and fast.
She follows it, the way you followed a heartbeat through a wall.
She climbs the steps two at a time. She trips. She gets up and keeps going.
[OUR GUESS] Our guess is that being found has never really needed energy. It only needs someone who won't stop looking.
She reaches the pillar. Her hand finds your jacket before her eyes find you.

## Morning (9:15)
[o nascer do sol sobre o santuário; Dani sentada ao lado; o scanner no chão a mostrar 0.00%]
You wake up on the stairs, with a jacket over you that isn't yours.
"You can't be found," Dani says, "unless someone really wants to."
You laugh, and it hurts, and you don't care.
The scanner on the step still says 0.00. It always will.
Born to hunt. Never meant to be found.
So I'll ask you: would you keep a body like that, if nobody could ever sense you again?
Tell me in the comments. And if you want the next one, tell me who you want to become.
