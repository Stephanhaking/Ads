## Cold open (0:00): prévia e promessa
[um quarto às 6:15; a tua sombra no chão não se mexe quando te mexes; cortes rápidos: cães pretos e brancos, um elefante a sair do chão]
You've woken up with Megumi's powers. Megumi Fushiguro, from Jujutsu Kaisen. In real life.
Your shadow isn't a shadow any more. It's a door, and things come out of it.
One of them will help you save a car park full of people.
Another has never been tamed by anyone but Sukuna, and in forty days you'll be tempted to open that one.
But first, it's 6:15 in the morning, and your shadow has just stopped copying you.

## 6:15 a.m.: the dogs (0:35)
[o quarto; a sombra do armário; dois cães, um branco e um preto, sentam-se]
You lift your arm. The shadow on the floor doesn't.
Then two dogs step out of it, one white, one black, and sit down in front of you like they've been waiting all night.
In the story these are the Divine Dogs, the first shikigami every user of the Ten Shadows Technique gets. They hunt curses.
Your cat, who has never respected anything, leaves the room.
Then the dogs find a smell in the corridor, and they take you with them. Your neighbour's door, the stairs, the bins, the bakery two streets away, where you arrive in pyjamas.
A dog can have up to about three hundred million scent receptors, against about six million in a human. That's the best case, not every dog, but it explains the bakery.
Lesson one: your shadow has a very good nose, and no idea what's yours.

## 12:30 p.m.: the toad (1:35)
[o escritório; o frigorífico da copa; um sapo do tamanho de uma pessoa]
At lunch you try the next one, as quietly as you can.
Something wet slaps out of your shadow and onto the office floor: a toad the size of a person.
In the story this is Toad, one of the shikigami. And in the story, two of them can even fuse into something new.
You hide it in the kitchen fridge. It has left a tongue's worth of mess on the printer.
Your boss asks who's been feeding the printer.
Lesson two: these things don't come with a manual, and they don't ask permission to appear.

## 6 p.m.: the elephant (2:15)
[uma garagem subterrânea vazia; uma sombra enorme; um elefante a sair do chão]
In the evening you take it to an empty underground car park, because you want to know where your limit is.
In the story, Max Elephant takes a huge amount of cursed energy to summon.
An adult African elephant weighs about six tonnes.
Our own maths: drop six thousand kilograms from three metres, and you get roughly one hundred and seventy-six thousand joules.
You summon it. You make it stand up. Then your legs go, and you sit on the concrete, shaking, because it took everything you had just to hold it there.
You spend the next hour finding where you left your car keys.
Lesson three: the bigger the shadow, the bigger the bill, and you're the one who pays.

## Day 3: the serpent (3:05)
[o corredor do prédio; uma serpente branca com marcas pretas]
On day three, you try the Great Serpent, a big white snake with black marks across its body.
The longest wild snake ever measured is a reticulated python of seven point two two metres, confirmed by Guinness in twenty twenty-six.
Yours fills the hallway. You can't tell where it ends.
Your neighbour opens her door, looks at the white coil blocking the stairs, and goes back inside without saying a word.
She never mentions it again, even when you meet in the lift.
Lesson four: when something is bigger than your building, you have to learn to put it back.

## Day 10: the world notices (3:45)
[um parque; um telemóvel a filmar; a tua sombra com forma estranha]
A boy in the park films your shadow at sunset. It's the wrong shape. It has ears, and it's wagging.
The video spreads. Half the comments say it's CGI. The other half say it's a ghost dog.
Nobody says "shikigami", because only a few people know what that means, and they think it's a joke.
Lesson five: the people who'd understand are the ones who'd never believe it.

## Day 15: the man in the suit (4:15)
[um café; um homem de fato com uma pasta; ele não pergunta nada]
A man in a grey suit sits down at your table in a café and doesn't ask a single question.
He slides a card across the table. He's not interested in what you can do. He wants to know what you'd refuse to do.
You say you don't know yet.
He nods, as if that was the right answer, and leaves without his coffee.
Lesson six: the first question anyone asks about power is how far you'd go.

## Day 30: the cost (4:55)
[a tua casa; estás sentado no chão; as sombras à volta]
By day thirty you can feel exactly how much each of them costs.
The dogs are cheap. The toad is cheap. The elephant leaves you in bed for a day.
You're starting to treat your own shadow like a bank account, and you're afraid to check the balance.
Lesson seven: you're never out of shadows. You're only ever out of you.

## Day 40: the car park (5:25)
[o desabamento de um parque de estacionamento; pessoas presas; poeiras]
On day forty, a floor of the car park near your flat collapses with eleven people underneath it.
You run. You're the only one who can do anything.
There's one thing in your shadow that could probably lift the slab with a single hand. Mahoraga.
In the story it's the strongest shikigami of the technique, and it adapts to whatever hits it; the wheel on its back turns as it learns.
But the ritual to summon it doesn't end until either its target is dead, or it's tamed. And in the story, the only person who ever tamed it was Sukuna.
You stand at the edge of the rubble with your hand over your shadow, and you take it away.
So you do the other thing. You spread your shadow across the floor, with no walls, no barrier, over whatever's already there. In the story, Megumi's version of this is called Chimera Shadow Garden, and it's incomplete.
Yours doesn't need to be perfect.
The dogs find them by smell, under the concrete. The elephant stands up under the slab and holds it, and your legs shake the whole time.
Eleven people come out. Nobody asks what you are.

## Day 41: the cost, again (6:30)
[o teu quarto; a sombra imóvel; tu, exausto]
The next morning you can't get out of bed.
Now you know what it really is. Not a gift. A door in the floor, and you're the only one who decides what comes through.
Lesson eight: the strongest thing in your shadow is the one you didn't open.

## Ending (6:45)
[6:15 a.m.; o quarto; a tua sombra no chão, parada]
The next morning at 6:15, you lift your arm, and your shadow doesn't move.
You wait. Something stirs under it, and it sits. It's the white dog.
So if you woke up tomorrow with Megumi's powers, which one would you call first?
And would you ever open the one that can only be stopped by killing you? Tell me in the comments.
