You've woken up as Toji Fushiguro. In real life. You have zero cursed energy. Nothing in this world can sense you. In the story, that isn't a weakness. It's the trade he was born with. In your version, there's a curse hunting your best friend tonight. You're the only thing it can't feel coming. But first, it's three oh seven in the morning, and your hands are shaking, because they are much stronger than they should be.

Toji was born with a Heavenly Restriction. It took away all of his cursed energy and gave him a body and senses at the limit. You sit up and the bed frame cracks. In the story, Toji was called the invisible man. Nothing could sense him, unless it came from the energy in his tools or a curse he carried. In your version, that's the whole trick. It doesn't stop. It doesn't hesitate. It simply drifts on, as if you were a piece of the wall. In your version, that's how the world sees you now. Not as an enemy. As nothing at all. You'd expected to feel powerful. You feel, mostly, a little lonely. Dani sees your face and says, gently: "It's not a bad thing. It's just a very quiet thing." Dani's mother left something in the storage unit under the shrine. A long black case. Four tools. You know what they are before you open it. The Inverted Spear of Heaven nullifies a technique the moment it touches it. In the story, it ended Gojo's Infinity. In your version, it only cancels what is active when it touches. It does nothing to a fist. It does nothing to a wall. The Chain of a Thousand Miles gives it reach, like a kusarigama. You swing it once, in an empty room. It takes the ceiling light with it. The Split Soul Katana cuts the soul and ignores the body's armor. In your version, it can hurt a curse without cutting a single brick. You test it on a mattress. The mattress is fine. You are not. And the Playful Cloud is a special grade tool. A heavy weapon. You pick it up one-handed. You're careful with this one too. Dani watches you. "Please tell me you're not going to do the thing." The curse is in the old shrine at the end of the line, where the metro turns around. You can feel it, because your body feels everything. A pulse in the cold, like a slow knock on glass. Dani stays at the gate with the scanner. Her screen shows a big red number for the curse. And for you, just a quiet grey frame that says NO DETECTION. In your version, that's the rule that makes you dangerous. Not strength. The fact that it never knows you're coming. You walk up the stairs in silence. Not a single sound. The curse is waiting at the top. It's turned toward the gate, toward Dani, toward all that warm, loud energy. It isn't looking at you. You're not there. Our guess is that a creature that hunts by sensing energy would never be built to expect something without any. You step into its blind spot, and for one second you are the quietest thing in the world.

Its first strike misses by an inch, because it's aiming at where a person's energy would be, and there isn't any. Its second misses by less. It's learning. Its third doesn't miss. It hits you. In your version, your body is a wall, not a god. It hurts. Something in your ribs says so out loud. You hit back with the katana, the one that ignores armor. Once. Twice. The curse falls apart without a sound. No dust. Nothing broken. Just gone. The red moon is very quiet. You stand there, breathing, waiting for the feeling you expected. Victory. Pride. Something. There's only the cold, and the tiredness, and the sound of Dani's footsteps stopping at the gate. Then your legs stop listening.

You fall at the foot of the torii, behind a pillar, and nobody knows. Dani is calling your name. Her scanner shows the curse is gone, and it shows nothing else. It shows no one. In the story, that's what made Toji so frightening. Nothing could find him. In your version, that's exactly what's about to kill you. Dani throws the scanner down. She stops trying to feel you. Our guess is that being found has never really needed energy. It only needs someone who won't stop looking. She reaches the pillar. Her hand finds your jacket before her eyes find you.

You wake up on the stairs, with a jacket over you that isn't yours. "You can't be found," Dani says, "unless someone really wants to." You laugh, and it hurts, and you don't care.
