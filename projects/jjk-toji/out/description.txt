WHAT'S LORE AND WHAT'S OURS
In the story (Jujutsu Kaisen, from secondary sources): Toji was born with a Heavenly Restriction that took away all of his cursed energy and gave him a body and senses at the limit; his senses can catch curses through tiny changes in the air and the temperature; he was called the invisible man, because nothing could sense him except the energy of his tools or of a curse he carried; the Zenin clan pushed him out for relying on tools instead of cursed energy; the Inverted Spear of Heaven nullifies a technique on contact and was used against Gojo's Infinity; the Chain of a Thousand Miles gives it reach, like a kusarigama; the Split Soul Katana cuts the soul and ignores the body's physical resistance; Playful Cloud is a special grade tool, a heavy weapon.
Our fiction: waking up in his body with your own face, one night, the curse hunting your best friend, the scanner and its numbers (0.00%, 64.2% and the rest), the spear only cancelling what is active, the katana cutting a curse without cutting a brick, the ward at the gate, the fight and how it ends, and every character and place in the video (Dani, her mother, the flat, the stairwell, the kitchen, the street, the storage unit, the shrine at the end of the line). The two lines labelled OUR GUESS are our own guesses, not facts.
