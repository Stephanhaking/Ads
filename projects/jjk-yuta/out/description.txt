WHAT'S LORE AND WHAT'S REAL
In the story (Jujutsu Kaisen / Jujutsu Kaisen 0): Rika Orimoto was Yuta's childhood friend; she gave him her mother's ring as a promise to marry him and died young; her soul stayed tied to his cursed energy and became a special-grade curse, the "Queen of Curses"; Yuta's technique, Copy, reproduces other people's techniques, and its exact conditions aren't fully known; in the film he uses Cursed Speech, the technique of Toge Inumaki's family, normally passed down only inside that clan; he calls Rika out with the ring, and her cursed energy is vast; after the film Rika's soul moves on and Yuta keeps using her power.
Real world: the study cited in the video is Albert Bandura's 1961 "Bobo doll" experiment at Stanford, in which children who watched an adult hit an inflatable doll later imitated it without being told to.
The scenes in the world (the omelette, the busker, the climbing wall, the dancer, the chess player, the man on the pavement, the flooded tunnel) and the "five skills at a time" limit are our own fiction, not part of the story. The pale figure in this video is our own drawing.
