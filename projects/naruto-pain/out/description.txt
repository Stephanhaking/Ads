WHAT'S LORE AND WHAT'S OURS
In the story (Naruto, from secondary sources): the Six Paths of Pain are six bodies, each with one Path (Deva, Animal, Preta, Asura, Naraka, Human), controlled from far away; the Rinnegan shares what every body sees with the user; the Deva Path controls gravity: Shinra Tensei pushes everything away and Banshō Ten'in pulls it in; after a Shinra Tensei there is a cooldown of at least five seconds, during which the Deva Path can't use its other abilities; the Animal Path summons creatures; the Asura Path modifies the body with mechanical parts; the Preta Path absorbs energy; the Human Path can pull a soul out of someone; the Naraka Path is described as the most valuable of the six, and the others protect it; if the controller is interrupted while sending chakra, every body stops and becomes vulnerable; Nagato's own body was weak, and the six bodies did the walking for him.
Our fiction: waking up as Pain with your own face and your own weak body, the six bodies as hollow vessels with nobody inside, the dam, the valley and the town, the deadline at midnight, the Preta taking the force out of a river, the stone room, the cables, the eight seconds and the choice at the end, and every character and place in the video (Ruth, the boy on the bicycle, the bakery, the school, the radio). The lines labelled OUR GUESS are our own guesses, not facts.
