WHAT'S LORE AND WHAT'S REAL
In the story (Jujutsu Kaisen): a Domain Expansion builds the user's innate domain inside a barrier, filled with their innate technique; inside it, attacks are guaranteed to hit (the "sure-hit" effect); it costs an immense amount of cursed energy; an incomplete domain loses the guaranteed hit; a Simple Domain can neutralise a domain's guaranteed hit; Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine has no closed barrier and works as an open space with a huge reach; Gojo's Unlimited Void floods the target with information until they can't move.
Real world: the study cited in the video is George Miller's 1956 paper in Psychological Review on the limits of short-term memory; people hold only around four to nine things in mind at once (the exact number is still debated).
Our fiction: the red threads (the hero's technique), the "no escape" rule, how the size grows, the calm yoga teacher, the dome that keeps things out, and every scene in the world (the kitchen, the lift, the meeting, the supermarket, the station, the car, the playground, the school in the storm). The dome and the characters in this video are our own drawings.
