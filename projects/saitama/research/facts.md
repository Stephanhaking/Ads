| ID | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|

| R02 | Speed of sound in air at 20 °C: about 343 m/s | standard physics value |
| C01 | 343 / 12 ≈ 29× faster; kinetic energy scales with speed squared → ≈ 800× the energy (same fist mass) | calculation |
