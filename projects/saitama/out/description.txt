What If You Had Saitama's Powers in Real Life? (One-Punch Man)

You wake up bald, and anything you hit with real intent goes down in one punch. Over 121 days you learn what that really means: a flat made of paper, a gym wall with a hole in it, a punch that breaks the sound barrier, a job interview, a falling crane, people in grey suits who can't measure you, and the one enemy you can't punch: boredom.

THE REAL FACTS IN THIS VIDEO (secondary sources and search summaries; if you spot an error, tell us)
- The fastest punches measured in labs reach about 12 m/s (lead hook, amateur boxers); sound travels at about 343 m/s, so breaking the sound barrier would take nearly 30 times that speed (our calculation)
- Stress hair loss (telogen effluvium) usually shows up 2–4 months after a big physical stress and usually grows back
- A daily push-up routine brings fast gains at first, then progress slows; months of the same high volume often lead to sore elbows or shoulders (coaching sources, not a controlled study)
- Escaping Earth's gravity takes about 11.2 km/s
- Hedonic adaptation: people get used to almost any change, good or bad, and the thrill fades (Sonja Lyubomirsky and others)
