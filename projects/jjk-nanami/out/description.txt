WHAT'S LORE AND WHAT'S OURS
In the story (Jujutsu Kaisen, from secondary sources): Nanami's Ratio Technique splits a target into ten parts, and a strike at the seven-to-three point creates a weak spot and lands as a critical hit; his weapon is a blunt sword that can still cut weaker things because of the technique; his power is bound by a vow (Overtime): it is limited during working hours and climbs a lot after hours; Collapse applies the seven-to-three line to the environment itself (a wall, a floor) so the whole place crumbles from one strike; he left sorcery for an ordinary office job and came back later.
Our fiction: waking up as him with your own face and name tag, the curse sealed inside an office building, the doors sealing at exactly 5:00 p.m., the working-hours version of the technique being enough to see lines but not to win a fight, the curse that feeds on exhaustion and is made of desks, cables and clocks, the overtime "loan" with an eleven-minute clock on your heart, the file room, the fire alarm, the glass arch, the time clock, the fight and how it ends, and every character and place in the video (Mr. Hale, Priya, the Henderson account, the office, the lobby, the stairwell). The suit, the yellow tie and the wristwatch are only the look of this video. The line labelled OUR GUESS is our own guess, not a fact.
