What If You Had Killua's Powers in Real Life? (Hunter x Hunter — electricity)

A storm takes the power out and you're stuck alone in a lift. You put your hand on the panel, a blue spark jumps into the metal, and the lights come back on. Over 40 days you find out what electricity can do inside a person: a water-and-leaf test, a first spark that hurts, an electric eel at the aquarium, a ball you catch before you decide to, a sprint you can't steer, a viral goal save, a favour you refuse, a battery that runs out, and a stopped Ferris wheel in a storm.

THE LORE (secondary sources)
- In the story, Nen comes in six types; Killua is a Transmuter who turns his aura into electricity (Wikipedia, Game Rant)
- Godspeed has two parts: Whirlwind (electrical signals sent straight to his muscles) and Speed of Lightning (Game Rant)
- His tolerance for electricity comes from childhood training in the Zoldyck family (CBR)
- In the story, Godspeed uses up his electricity fast (one source: Game Rant)

THE REAL SCIENCE AND WHERE IT COMES FROM
- Volta's electric eel (Electrophorus voltai), described in 2019 by de Santana and colleagues, can discharge up to 860 V, more than the roughly 650 V of the better-known electric eel (Wikipedia; ScienceDaily, 10 Sep 2019)
- A person needs about a quarter of a second to react to something they see, roughly 190 to 285 milliseconds (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, 2015)
