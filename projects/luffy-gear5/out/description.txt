THE LORE (secondary sources: fan wikis and chapter coverage; check against the manga)
- In chapter 1044 (2022), Luffy's fruit is revealed as the Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika
- Gear 5: a rubber body that deforms freely and can give rubber properties to things around it
- After Gear 5, Luffy's stamina runs out and he looks decades older for a moment (chapter 1045)
- Gear 2 pumps blood faster; Gear 3 inflates his bones and early on shrank him afterwards; Gear 4 leaves him without Haki for about 10 minutes
- Luffy's rubber body is immune to Enel's lightning (Skypiea)

THE REAL SCIENCE AND WHERE IT COMES FROM
- Natural rubber stretches to roughly 3 to 9 times its length before breaking (elongation at break about 300–900 %, depending on the compound; supplier datasheets, ASTM D412)
- The Gough-Joule effect: rubber warms when stretched and cools when released (John Gough, 1802; studied by James Joule in the 1850s)
- Natural rubber's glass transition is about −55 to −72 °C; below it, it turns stiff and brittle
- A typical lightning flash: about 300 million volts and 30,000 amps (US National Weather Service)
- Class 4 electricians' rubber gloves are rated for up to 36,000 V, so lightning is about 8,300 times more (our calculation)
- Above a high enough voltage, any insulator suddenly conducts (dielectric breakdown)
- Car tyres don't protect you from lightning; the metal body carries the current around you (NWS)
- Lightning heats the air to about 30,000 °C, around five times the Sun's visible surface (NWS / NOAA)
- Estimated maximum heart rate is about 220 minus your age, a rough rule that can be off by 10–20 bpm

These figures come from secondary sources and search summaries; not every original paper was read. If you spot an error, tell us in the comments.
