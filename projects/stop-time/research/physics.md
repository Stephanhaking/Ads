# Physics ledger: What if you could stop time?

Premise (fiction, story only): the hero can freeze time for the world while she keeps moving. Physicists point out that a
global stop is not self-consistent; the story uses a "bubble" (a small region around her where time still runs) and says so.
Inputs come from web-search summaries (secondary sources); originals not all opened.
