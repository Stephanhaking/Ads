What If You Had Star Platinum in Real Life? (Jotaro, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure)

You wake up at 7:12, a glass slides off your nightstand, and a huge blue-and-purple arm comes out of your shoulder and catches it. Over 40 days you find out what it means to own the fastest pair of hands in the world: a tower of tins in a supermarket, a bent bar at the gym, a heavy bag that lasts four seconds, keys rescued from a drain, a viral football save, a whiteboard full of numbers, and a van rolling toward a school crossing.

THE LORE (secondary sources)
- In the story, Star Platinum's base powers are strength, speed and precision, far beyond a human (Wikipedia; CBR)
- Its signature barrage is "ORA ORA" (Game Rant); Star Finger stretches two fingers to reach further (CBR)
- Near the end of Part 3, Jotaro gets the same time-stop power as The World, about 5 seconds at first (Wikipedia)

THE REAL NUMBERS AND WHERE THEY COME FROM
- One of the heaviest deadlifts ever recorded: about 510 kg, Hafthor Bjornsson, 2025 (Guinness World Records)
- Boxers' punches move at roughly 6 to 11 metres per second in studies (PMC; Frontiers)
- Human visual reaction time is about 190 to 285 milliseconds, roughly a quarter of a second (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, 2015)
- A 9 mm pistol bullet typically leaves the barrel at around 350 to 410 m/s (Wikipedia)
- Usain Bolt averaged about 10 m/s in his 9.58 s 100 m (Wikipedia)
- Our own maths: at 360 m/s, 10 m takes about 28 ms, about nine times shorter than a 250 ms reaction. "Twenty punches a second" is only an example, not a number from the anime.
